Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The templates work by pasting your token address. For anything else, your AI writes the code; vibekit makes sure it uses the right contracts and rules.

Is it safe?

vibekit never asks for a private key and never sends anything. Everything is Robinhood Chain testnet, where tokens have no real value. Never paste a real wallet's private key into any AI or website.

What is MCP?

A standard way to give an AI assistant extra tools. Adding the vibekit address lets your AI look up live vibe/vibe data (tokens, trades, holders, guilds) while it builds.

Is this made by vibe/vibe?

No. vibekit is an unofficial community toolkit by @0xHaileyy, built from vibe/vibe's public contracts and data. The code is open source.
